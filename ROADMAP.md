# Roadmap

## G0.0 Project Bootstrap
Verified build, emulator, input and native frame-capture pipeline. First Grindungeonia ROM boots to a development screen.

## G0 Combat Room
Prove that Grindungeonia can feel like a tiny GBA loot/buildcraft ARPG before building the larger game.

Acceptance target:
- One controllable player with readable movement/facing.
- One representative primary active skill and its weapon/attack presentation.
- A small enemy pack, not merely a duel target.
- HP/resource UI, damage, hit feedback, enemy death and player death/restart.
- Enemy drops with at least one generated equipment item.
- Player can inspect/equip the drop and produce an immediately observable combat-stat change.
- Representative dungeon-room presentation and basic combat VFX.
- Native 240x160 captures verify idle, combat, kill/drop and equipped-improvement states.

Dodge is not a mandatory pillar. Keep it only if testing shows it improves the GBA ARPG combat loop.

## G0.5 Loot/Build Vertical Slice
Turn the proof into the first repeatable loop: multiple equipment bases, compact affix generation, rarity, inventory/equipment handling, at least two active-skill behaviors, and a tiny passive-choice prototype.

## G1 First Dungeon Run
A short multi-room/floor run with enemy families, loot progression, a boss/Guardian, death/restart flow and a persistent character progression record.

## Later
Expand skill/support interactions, passive network, itemization, crafting, defenses/damage types, ailments, bosses and tier progression incrementally. Do not front-load the entire long-term ARPG system list.
